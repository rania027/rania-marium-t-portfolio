## Brand & Style

This design system expresses an avant-garde academic aesthetic created for a specialized developer portfolio in AI & Machine Learning. It reconciles two seemingly opposing domains: classical Renaissance intellectual tradition and precision-engineered modern computation. 

The visual identity relies on intellectual restraint, tactile warmth, and deliberate structure. Rather than leaning into generic silicon-valley high-tech tropes—such as neon accents, dark terminal matrices, or glossy glassmorphism—it draws from classic scholarly journals, archival folios, and natural materials.

### Core Tenets
- **Scholarly Authority:** Employs deliberate, elegant serif typography for major titles and statements, framing technical research and code with literary seriousness.
- **Precision Engineering:** Uses sharp, technical grotesques and monospaced indicators for metadata, architecture diagrams, and parameters, ensuring effortless readability for engineering-focused artifacts.
- **Organic Materiality:** Grounds the screen in warm ivory, linen, deep olive, and sun-baked terracotta rather than sterile cold grays, evoking linen paper, natural pigments, and tactile bindings.

## Colors

The palette establishes an organic, high-contrast, light-mode environment reminiscent of archival print media infused with earthy mineral tones.

### Color Roles & Mapping
- **Primary (`#C58F78` — Muted Terracotta):** Used selectively for highlights, focus states, interactive underlines, active badges, and specialized AI/ML focus markers. It acts as the spark of warmth across the neutral canvas.
- **Secondary (`#35483B` — Deep Olive Green):** Applied to primary actions, anchored brand symbols, high-contrast badges, and primary interactive buttons. Provides grounding weight and structural contrast.
- **Tertiary (`#A8B5A0` — Muted Sage):** Deployed for subtle indicators, secondary tags, structural wash backgrounds, and pill status badges for completed or validated research items.
- **Neutral Surface & Base:**
  - Canvas Background: `#F7F5EF` (Warm Ivory)
  - Secondary Canvas / Surface Container: `#EEEAE0` (Soft Linen)
  - Structural Hairlines & Borders: `#DCDDD2` (Bone Chalk)
- **Neutral Content:**
  - Primary Text & Display Headings: `#292C27` (Warm Charcoal)
  - Secondary Text, Captions & Byline: `#70736A` (Weathered Stone)
  - Disabled & Muted Text: `#A6A89E`

### Usage Rules
Avoid pure black (`#000000`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) anywhere across surfaces or typography to preserve the organic folio tactile feel. Maintain high legibility by rendering all continuous long-form copy in `#292C27` against `#F7F5EF` or `#EEEAE0`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy intentionally contrasts historical literary gravitas with modern computational rigor:

- **Headlines (`EB Garamond`):** Renders editorial titling, publication names, biographical introductions, and thesis headers. It confers a distinguished, academic tone.
- **Body & Interface (`Geist`):** Delivers clean readability for descriptive paragraphs, technical summaries, documentation, project abstracts, and navigation items.
- **Labels & Micro-data:** Set in uppercase or tracking-expanded `Geist` at `label-sm` and `label-md` to maintain clinical clarity for technical metrics, model hyperparameters, dates, and classification tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in classical editorial proportion, anchored by an outer boundary constrained to a comfortable `max-w-6xl` (72rem / 1152px) container.

### Layout Mechanics
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column layout on desktop screens with standard `1.5rem` gutters. Collapses to an 8-column grid on tablet devices and a 4-column column on mobile viewports.
- **Rhythm & Whitespace:** Generous vertical rhythm separates research pillars, showcased machine learning architectures, and academic experience. Section breaks use `space-2xl` (5rem) or greater to allow dense technical artifacts space to breathe.
- **Alignment:** Asymmetric editorial balancing—large Garamond headlines occupy left spans (columns 1–5), while technical synopses, schematics, and live links align to the remaining columns (6–12).

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids heavy drop shadows and translucent glossy blurs, opting instead for structural depth through surface layering and hairline borders.

### Elevation Principles
- **Atmospheric Planarity:** Flat surfaces layer on top of one another. The ground plane is Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EF`). Interactive cards, code manifests, and project panels sit on Soft Linen (`#EEEAE0`).
- **Hairline Framing:** Depth and separation are achieved primarily via 1px crisp borders in `#DCDDD2`. 
- **Subtle Ambient Hover:** When an interactive project tile or paper link is engaged, avoid dramatic lift. Instead, use a microscopic, tinted ambient shadow combined with a subtle border tint shift:
  - `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(41, 44, 39, 0.05)`
  - Border transitions smoothly from `#DCDDD2` to `#A8B5A0` or `#C58F78`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary emphasizes architectural precision and restrained discipline.

### Geometry & Radius
- **Base Corner Radius (`roundedness: 1`):** Base components, chips, and small controls use `0.25rem` (4px).
- **Cards & Architectural Containers:** Surface blocks and modal sheets use `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px) or `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px) to softly catch the eye without softening into generic consumer app circularity.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted strictly to categorical tags and status indicators (e.g., "Active Model", "Published Paper", "PyTorch") where complete pill rounding clarifies metadata status.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Button:** Filled with Deep Olive (`#35483B`), foreground Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EF`). Micro-radius (`roundedness: 1`). Monospaced or uppercase `label-md` Geist typography. Hover transitions background to a soft, rich olive blend with zero vertical displacement.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, 1px solid `#DCDDD2` border, foreground Warm Charcoal (`#292C27`). Hover brings a gentle background fill of Soft Linen (`#EEEAE0`) and a border shift to `#A8B5A0`.
- **Editorial Action Link:** Bare text link featuring a continuous 1px bottom underline tinted with Terracotta (`#C58F78`) offset by 3px. On hover, the underline thickens to 1.5px.

### Cards & Project Folios
- Background: Soft Linen (`#EEEAE0`).
- Border: 1px solid `#DCDDD2`.
- Padding: `space-lg` (1.75rem) internal padding.
- Structure: Split view featuring an editorial headline (`EB Garamond`), technical classification badges on the upper right, body description (`Geist`), and footer technical specifications (e.g., parameters, frameworks, dataset sizes).

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Height: Compact 24px–28px.
- Background: `#F7F5EF` or `#E5E0D5`.
- Border: 1px solid `#DCDDD2`.
- Text: `label-sm` in Warm Charcoal (`#292C27`) or Deep Olive (`#35483B`).
- Terracotta Variant: Reserved for highlighting core ML fields (e.g., "Computer Vision", "LLM Evaluation").

### Lists & Research Indices
- Horizontal rows divided by 1px solid `#DCDDD2` borders.
- Monospaced numbering (`01`, `02`, `03`) on the left in Muted Stone (`#70736A`), project/paper title in `headline-sm` (`EB Garamond`), and tags flushed right.
- Hover changes the row background subtly to `#EEEAE0`.

### Form Inputs & Code Blocks
- **Input Fields:** Unfilled or Soft Linen background, 1px border `#DCDDD2`. Active focus state drops inner ring; instead, shifts border cleanly to Terracotta (`#C58F78`).
- **Code & Metric Blocks:** Contained within `#292C27` dark fill or `#EBE7DC` warm light fill, inset borders, and precise monospaced readouts for architectural diagrams and performance benchmarks.
